Healing Protocol

Tattoo Aftercare Guide

Proper aftercare accounts for 50% of your final tattoo quality. Follow these strict studio guidelines.

• Keep the protective wrap on for 3–5 hours (or up to 24 hours if using second-skin bandage like Saniderm).

• Wash gently with warm water and fragrance-free antibacterial soap using clean hands only.

• Pat dry with a clean paper towel—never rub with a cloth towel.

• Apply a ultra-thin layer of unscented moisturizer (such as Aquaphor or specialized tattoo ointment) 2-3 times daily.

• DO NOT PICK OR SCRATCH: Flaking skin is normal. Picking scabs causes ink fallout and scarring.

• Wear loose-fitting cotton clothing to prevent friction against the healing skin.

• No Submersion: Avoid baths, hot tubs, pools, lakes, and oceans for at least 2-3 weeks.

• No Direct Sun: Keep the tattoo out of direct sunlight while it is healing.

• No Intense Workouts: Avoid heavy sweating or tight workout gear over the tattoo area for the first 5-7 days.

• Always apply broad-spectrum SPF 50+ sunscreen once healed to prevent ink oxidation and fading.

• We offer one complimentary touch-up within 3 months of your appointment date provided proper aftercare was followed.
